What is Google Ads and how can it help my business?

Google Ads lets you show ads to people who are actively searching for the products or services you offer. Because the search already signals intent, it suits businesses that want enquiries from people who are ready to act. We handle keyword and intent research, campaign structure, ad copy, location targeting and conversion tracking.

What is Local SEO?

Local SEO is the work that helps your business appear when people search for relevant products and services in your area. It covers your Google Business Profile, local keywords, consistent business information, citations, reviews, location pages and on-page SEO.

What is Google Business Profile optimisation?

Your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is what people see in Google Search and Google Maps. Optimising it means choosing the right categories, completing your business information and services, writing a clear description, keeping hours and contact details accurate, adding photos and building a workflow for reviews.

Can you help improve our Google Maps visibility?

We can optimise your Google Business Profile and local search presence to improve your visibility for relevant local searches. Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, so the work focuses on those signals.

Do you guarantee Google Maps or search rankings?

No, and you should be cautious of any agency that does. Google states that a better organic local ranking cannot be bought or requested, and that local results are based primarily on relevance, distance and prominence. Our objective is to improve your relevance, completeness, visibility and overall local search presence through legitimate optimisation.

What is the difference between Google Ads and SEO?

Google Ads buys visibility: it is campaign-based, budget-controlled and can start quickly. SEO earns visibility: it is a longer-term mix of technical work, content and relevance. Local search through your Business Profile and Maps is a third path, driven by business information, reviews and location. They do different jobs and often work best together.
